Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-29. Written in plain language and being reviewed by our lawyers; we’ll ask you to accept any new version the next time you sign in.

This page says what we save when you visit Safekeep, sign up, or practice with us, and how to ask us to remove it. The short version is that we keep what we need to run your subscription and send practice you’ve agreed to, we don’t sell it, and you can ask us to delete it by replying to any email from us.

When you fill in the sign-up form

If you sign up, we save your first name, email address, whether you want help for yourself or someone else, any first name, email, or phone number you optionally provide for that person, your optional phone number, the plan and billing period you choose, signup time, and the contact notice shown when you join using our form provider, Formspree, which stores them and emails them to the Safekeep team. Only the Safekeep team uses these details, to set up and run your subscription. Your submission also includes the campaign tags from the link you followed, your device category, and a random session identifier, so we can see which ads lead to signups.

When you create an account

Your email address, your password, and your acceptance of the Terms of Service and this policy are stored by our account provider, Supabase. Your password is never visible to the Safekeep team. We also record when you accepted the terms, which version, the kind of browser you used, and a scrambled form of your internet address, so we can show that the agreement happened. Your profile (your name and time zone, and a date of birth only if you choose to give one) and the phone numbers you add are stored in the same place.

When you pay

Payments are handled by Stripe, which collects your card details on its own secure checkout page. Safekeep never sees your full card number. We keep a record of which plan you have and whether it’s active, and we use your email to send receipts and account updates.

Practice messages and your choices

Signing up does not start automated calls or text messages. Practice only goes to a person who has agreed to it on that channel, and we keep a record of each time someone agrees or withdraws, so we can prove we had permission. If someone pays for a plan for you, they can see which channels you chose and how your practice is going in broad terms; they can’t see the content of your practice messages, your phone number, or your schedule.

Measuring our ads

This site uses the Google tag to measure how our Google ads perform: Google receives information about your visit, and when you sign up we tell Google that a signup happened. We don’t send Google your name, email address, or phone number. This site does not add Meta or TikTok tracking pixels. Hosting providers may process ordinary request logs, and fonts load from Google Fonts.

Who we share with

Formspree, Supabase, Stripe, Google (for ad measurement), and our hosting provider, Netlify, each handle part of the data described above in order to provide their service to us. We don’t sell your information and we don’t share it with anyone else, unless the law requires us to.

Deleting your details

You can ask us to delete your details at any time by replying to any Safekeep email. Sign-ups that are never verified are removed automatically after 30 days. Self-service deletion and a copy of your data are on the way; until then, a person on the team handles each request.

Contact

Reply to any email from Safekeep, or write to the address on your receipt. A person will answer.
