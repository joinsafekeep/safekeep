Safekeep is run by the Safekeep team (“we,” “us”). When you create an account or pay for a plan, you’re agreeing to the terms below. If you have a question about any of them, reply to any email from us and a person will answer.

What Safekeep is, and isn’t

Safekeep sends realistic practice scams to people who have agreed to receive them, then shows them what the warning signs were. It’s educational practice. Scams change constantly and some are very convincing, so we can’t promise that someone who practices with Safekeep will never be fooled, and we don’t guarantee protection against fraud or any loss. If you think a real scam is under way, stop, don’t send money or codes, and use the resources under “I think I’ve been scammed” on our homepage.

Who can use it

You have to be 18 or older to create an account, and everyone who receives practice messages has to be an adult who has agreed to receive them. When you sign up, you confirm that you’re at least 18. You’re responsible for keeping your password private.

Practice messages need the participant’s permission

We never send a practice email, text, or call to someone who hasn’t agreed to receive it themselves, on that channel. Paying for a plan for someone else doesn’t give you the right to agree on their behalf. They get an invitation, they decide, and they can stop at any time from their own account. The practice messages are made up: no real money is ever requested, and we never collect passwords, card numbers, or account details through them.

Plans, billing, and refunds

Plans are billed monthly or annually through Stripe, which handles your card details on its own secure page. Monthly plans can be canceled at any time, and the plan runs to the end of the period you’ve paid for. Annual plans keep access through the paid year. Every plan comes with a 60-day money-back guarantee: if Safekeep isn’t right for you, ask for a refund within 60 days of your first payment and we’ll give you a full one. Prices are shown on the pricing page; if we change them, your current plan keeps its price until it next renews, and we’ll tell you beforehand.

Your account and your data

How we handle your information is set out in the Privacy Policy. You can ask us to delete your details by replying to any Safekeep email. If you close your account, practice stops right away.

Being fair to each other

Please don’t use Safekeep to harass anyone, to send practice to people who haven’t agreed, or to try to break into our systems. We may close an account that does. We may change Safekeep over time, and we’ll give notice before any change that affects what you’ve paid for.

Limits on our responsibility

Safekeep is provided as it is. To the extent the law allows, we aren’t liable for losses that come from a real scam, from relying on practice content, or from interruptions to the service. Where the law doesn’t allow us to limit responsibility, we don’t. Nothing in these terms takes away rights you have as a consumer.
