Pricing questions

Which plan is right for me?

Individual is for one person. Pairs puts two people on one subscription, and Family covers up to four. Each participant gets their own simulations, coaching, and reports.

How is this different from a scam blocker?

Safekeep helps people practice recognizing scams that reach them. Simulations and feedback build awareness; the monthly report shows progress and highlights new scams.

When can I get started?

Join the free waitlist and we’ll email you when enrollment opens. There’s no payment or commitment today.

What if my loved one doesn’t want to participate?

Everyone chooses whether to take part. We won’t send simulations without their consent. Once subscribed, every plan includes a 60-day full-refund guarantee.

Can I cancel?

Monthly plans can be canceled anytime. With annual billing, access continues through the paid year. The 60-day money-back guarantee applies to both billing options.
