## How to use this

- Put the lead term in the seo_title, the H1 or first heading, and the first sentence of the quick answer, worded naturally.
- A topic with low search numbers can still be worth writing if readers need it; just lead with a term people actually search.
- Refresh these numbers every few months from Keyword Planner (Tools, Planning, Keyword Planner, "Get search volume and forecasts"). Search Console will show which terms the pages actually appear for once it's set up.
